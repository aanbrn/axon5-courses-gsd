  <files>scripts/smoke-stack.sh</files>
  <action>
    Create scripts/smoke-stack.sh as an executable bash script implementing the RESEARCH.md § "scripts/smoke-stack.sh" shape but with a PORTABLE process-group launch (do NOT copy `setsid`, which does not exist on macOS/darwin): `set -euo pipefail`, `set -m` (enable job control so the backgrounded Gradle gets its own process group whose PGID equals `$!`), a `cleanup` trap that runs `kill -- "-${RUN_PID}" 2>/dev/null || true` and `docker compose down -v --remove-orphans`, `docker compose up -d --wait`, `mkdir -p build`, `./gradlew bootRun --parallel --no-daemon --console=plain > build/bootrun.log 2>&1 &` then `RUN_PID=$!`, then poll each of ports 8081/8082/8080 on `/actuator/health` (up to 60 × 2s each) and finally `curl -fsS http://localhost:8080/ | grep -q '<div id="root">'`. `chmod +x scripts/smoke-stack.sh`. The `--no-daemon` flag keeps the Gradle client, the bootRun tasks, and their three JVMs in the one process group the `set -m` + `kill -- "-${RUN_PID}"` pattern reaps — without it the daemon forks the JVMs outside the group and the "no orphan" guarantee is false. The `/actuator/health` polls prove each service is UP (liveness); they deliberately do NOT prove Axon registration, because this starter combination registers no Axon actuator health indicator (RESEARCH § P5) — registration is proven by `./gradlew integrationTest --tests '*AxonServerRegistrationIT'`, wired into this plan's phase verification.
  </action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - `test -x scripts/smoke-stack.sh`.
    - `./scripts/smoke-stack.sh` exits 0 and prints "port 8081: up", "port 8082: up", "port 8080: up", and "SPA shell served by api".
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <verify>
    <automated>./scripts/smoke-stack.sh</automated>
